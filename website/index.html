How to install on Android
  1. Tap Download for Android. Chrome may warn that this type of file can harm your device — it says that for every app installed outside the Play Store. Choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded .apk file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open, and sign in to get started.

GoDoctor for iOS

iPhone and iPad

Your iPhone
Get GoDoctor for iOS

No App Store needed. Open GoDoctor, then add it to your Home Screen — it opens full screen with its own icon, just like any other app.

How to add it to your Home Screen

In Safari

  1. Tap Get GoDoctor for iOS above.
  2. Tap ••• at the bottom of the screen, then Share. On older iPhones, tap the Share button ↑ straight away.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep Open as Web App on if you see it, then tap Add. GoDoctor is now on your Home Screen.

In Chrome

  1. Tap the Share button ↑ in the address bar, at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen (scroll down, or tap More, if you don't see it), then Add.

Opened this page from WhatsApp, Instagram or another app? Open it in Safari first: tap ••• and choose Open in Safari.
