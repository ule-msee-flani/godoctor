Installing on Android

  1. Tap Download above. Chrome may warn that this file type can harm your device — that warning shows for every app installed outside the Play Store. Choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded .apk file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Android will ask to allow installs from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. Sign in with your phone number to get started.
